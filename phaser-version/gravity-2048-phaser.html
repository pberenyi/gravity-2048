Blocks merge only when the falling block lands on an identical value. A full row clears. Clearing a row triggers gravity, which can cause further merges and clears.

Bomb: Earn one when a block reaches row 11 from the bottom, a second at row 16. Press B to activate. Move with arrows, press Space on an empty cell to detonate - clears its row ± 1. Falls off the bottom = lost.
